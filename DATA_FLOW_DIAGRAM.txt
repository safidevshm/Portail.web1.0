PHONE NORMALIZATION MATRIX
──────────────────────────

Input Format              | Processing                    | Output
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
"06 66 94 71 66"         | Remove spaces                 | "0666947166"
"06-66-94-71-66"         | Remove dashes                 | "0666947166"
"+212 666 947 166"       | Remove +, convert 212→0       | "0666947166"
"00212666947166"         | Remove 00, convert 212→0      | "0666947166"
"212666947166"           | Convert leading 212→0         | "0666947166"
"666947166"              | Add leading 0                 | "0666947166"
"(212) 666-94-71-66"     | Remove (, ), -, convert       | "0266947166"
" 06 66 94 71 66 "       | Trim, remove spaces           | "0666947166"

Invalid: "0466947166"    → null ❌ (Invalid prefix 04)
Invalid: "333666947166"  → null ❌ (Invalid prefix 33)
Invalid: "+33666947166"  → null ❌ (French, not Moroccan)


TEXT NORMALIZATION MATRIX
─────────────────────────

Input                     | Processing                    | Output
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
"  John  "               | Trim                          | "John"
"John   Doe"            | Reduce multiple spaces        | "John Doe"
"  Jean   Paul   Smith " | Trim + reduce spaces         | "Jean Paul Smith"
"محمد  علي"            | Trim + reduce spaces         | "محمد علي"
"test\nline"            | Convert control char→space    | "test line"
"test\ttab"             | Convert tab→space             | "test tab"
"test\u00A0nbsp"       | Convert non-breaking space    | "test nbsp"
"   "                   | All spaces → nothing          | ""
"123456"                | Trim only (no spaces)         | "123456"
